CCC course guide: what it is, who can apply and how the exam works

Last reviewed: 22 October 2026 हिंदी में पढ़ें

CCC is NIELIT's basic computer course. Anyone can take it, whatever their education. Here is what you learn and how the exam and certificate work.

CCC stands for Course on Computer Concepts. Its exam is conducted by NIELIT (National Institute of Electronics and Information Technology), a Government of India organisation. CCC teaches the computer basics that most office and many government jobs expect.

What you learn

  • Introduction to computers and using a computer screen (GUI)
  • Word processing — typing and formatting documents
  • Spreadsheets — simple tables and calculations
  • Internet, the web, browsers and email
  • Communication and collaboration online
  • Making small presentations

Duration

NIELIT gives the total duration as 80 hours: 25 hours of theory, 5 hours of tutorials and 50 hours of practical work.

Who can apply

NIELIT says CCC is open irrespective of educational qualification. You can take it in one of three ways:

  1. Through an institute approved by NIELIT for CCC.
  2. Through a government-recognised school or college that has a NIELIT ID.
  3. As a direct candidate, after studying on your own.

Exam and certificate

  • Exams are held every month.
  • You apply online on NIELIT's student portal, where you also get your admit card and result.
  • After passing, you download a digitally signed certificate from NIELIT.
  • An exam fee applies. Check the current fee on NIELIT's website before applying.

Is CCC useful?

Basic computer skills help in almost every job today, from data entry and office work to shops and banks. Some job notifications ask for a basic computer certificate — read each notification carefully to see exactly what it asks for. After CCC, NIELIT also offers a longer IT course called O Level.

Tips

  • Practise typing every day; speed and accuracy matter in many jobs.
  • Only apply on NIELIT's official website. Do not pay agents.
