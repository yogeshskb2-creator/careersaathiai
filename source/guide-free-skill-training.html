Free government skill training in India: PMKVY, DDU-GKY, KVK and more

Last reviewed: 22 October 2026 हिंदी में पढ़ें

Want to learn a skill without spending much? These government programmes offer free or low-cost training, from job-role courses to farm training and online classes.

Learning a new skill is one of the surest ways to improve your job chances. Several government programmes offer training free of cost. Here are the main ones and who they are for.

PMKVY — Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana

PMKVY is the flagship skill-training scheme of the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, run through the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC). The current version is PMKVY 4.0.

  • For: unemployed youth and school or college dropouts, with special support for some groups.
  • Cost: training is free — the scheme pays the training cost.
  • Courses: short-term job-role courses, typically 300–600 hours, with on-the-job training.
  • Already skilled? Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) can certify skills you already have.
  • Certificate: nationally recognised, after an assessment.

DDU-GKY — for rural youth

Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana links skill training with jobs for young people from poor rural families.

  • For: rural youth aged 15–35 from poor (BPL) households, with priority for SC/ST, minorities, women and persons with disabilities.
  • Cost: free training; residential training is available.
  • After training: help with getting placed in a job.
  • How: register through your State skill mission or a DDU-GKY training partner.

Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) — farm and allied skills

KVKs, under ICAR, run short and long vocational training for farmers and rural youth in agriculture and allied work such as dairy and nursery. Contact the KVK in your district for upcoming trainings.

ITI trade courses

Government and private ITIs teach trades such as electrician, fitter and welder under DGT's Craftsmen Training Scheme. Courses last about 6 months to 2 years depending on the trade.

Free online courses: SWAYAM and NPTEL

  • SWAYAM, from the Ministry of Education, offers university-level courses free to learn. A certificate needs a paid, in-person proctored exam.
  • NPTEL, run by IITs and IISc, offers free course material in subjects like programming and data analytics, with a fee for the certification exam.

Choosing the right option

  • Want a job quickly? Look at PMKVY job-role courses or DDU-GKY.
  • Want a trade? Look at ITI.
  • Studying or working? Online courses let you learn at your own pace.
  • Finishing a course does not guarantee a job — but it builds real skills and gives you proof of them.
