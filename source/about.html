About CareerSaathi AI

CareerSaathi AI is a free guidance tool that helps people in India understand which careers, government recruitment sources, schemes and courses may suit their education and skills.

What this website does

  • Compares your profile with a small, hand-checked list of careers.
  • Explains why each career is suggested and which skills you may need.
  • Links you to official government websites for recruitment and schemes.
  • Suggests courses, preferring free and official options.

What this website does not do

  • It does not offer jobs or collect job applications.
  • It is not a live job feed. Our list of open recruitments is updated by hand and may miss some — always check official websites.
  • It does not approve schemes, loans, subsidies or benefits.
  • It is not connected to any government department.

What our labels mean

  • ◆ Potentially suitable career

    Your profile matches this career in general. It is an idea to explore.

  • ? Eligibility not yet verified

    We do not have all the official rules (for example age limits). Check the official notification.

  • ✓ Eligible based on available recorded criteria

    You meet the rules we have recorded. Official rules may have more conditions.

  • ★ Verified active vacancy

    Shown only when we checked a recruitment against its official notice and it is open on today's date.

Disclaimer

  • Information on this website is for general guidance only. It may be incomplete or out of date.
  • Always read the official notification or scheme guidelines before applying. Official rules always win.
  • A recommendation is not a promise of a job, admission, loan or benefit.
  • This is a prototype. Records marked "Demo data" are examples and must not be used for real decisions.
  • Beware of anyone asking for money for government jobs. Official recruitment never needs a bribe or an agent.
