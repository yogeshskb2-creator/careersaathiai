Privacy Information

Short version: we do not collect your personal data. Your answers are processed on your own device.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

What we ask for

  • Education, skills, experience and career goals — to suggest careers.
  • State and district — to show relevant state resources.
  • Your name is optional. You can use the site without it.

What we never ask for

  • Aadhaar, PAN or any ID number
  • Bank account or card details
  • Passwords or OTPs
  • Phone number, email or photos

Where your answers are kept

  • Your answers are processed inside your browser. There is no CareerSaathi server or database.
  • If you choose "Remember on this device", answers are saved only in this browser. You can delete them anytime.
  • We do not share anything with employers, agencies or advertisers.
  • This website does not use analytics or tracking tools. It does not show ads at the moment.
  • If you do not tick "Remember", your answers are kept only until you close the browser tab.
  • Interest-quiz answers are saved only in this browser so you can see your result again. "Delete my saved data" removes them too.
  • Private Job Finder answers are kept only in this browser (deleted when you close the tab, unless you choose "Remember"). "Delete my saved data" removes them too.
  • Calendar reminders are files made on your device. We never see them, and we cannot send you messages.

Outside services

  • The page font is loaded from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address, like any website you visit.
  • When you click an official link (for example ssc.gov.in), that website's own privacy policy applies.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

To keep CareerSaathi AI free, we may show ads from Google AdSense in future. When ads are shown, the following applies:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or to other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies lets Google and its partners show you ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Ad networks may also use cookies and similar technology to measure ads and prevent fraud.
  • Ad network we use: Google AdSense. If we add any other ad network, we will list it here.

Your choices about ads

  • Turn off personalised ads from Google in Google Ads Settings
  • Opt out of personalised-ad cookies from some other vendors at aboutads.info
  • Learn how Google uses information from websites that use its services: How Google uses this information

Your career profile is never shared with Google or any advertiser. It stays in your browser.

We do not choose the individual ads you see, and an ad is not our recommendation. Government jobs are never sold — ignore any ad that offers a job for money.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to us using the Contact page.
