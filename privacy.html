Privacy Information

Short version: we do not collect your personal data. Your answers are processed on your own device.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

What we ask for

  • Education, skills, experience and career goals — to suggest careers.
  • State and district — to show relevant state resources.
  • Your name is optional. You can use the site without it.

What we never ask for

  • Aadhaar, PAN or any ID number
  • Bank account or card details
  • Passwords or OTPs
  • Phone number, email or photos

Where your answers are kept

  • Your answers are processed inside your browser. There is no CareerSaathi server or database.
  • If you choose "Remember on this device", answers are saved only in this browser. You can delete them anytime.
  • We do not share anything with employers, agencies or advertisers.
  • This version has no ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • If you do not tick "Remember", your answers are kept only until you close the browser tab.

Outside services

  • The page font is loaded from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address, like any website you visit.
  • When you click an official link (for example ssc.gov.in), that website's own privacy policy applies.

Delete data saved on this device

This removes your saved language choice and any saved profile from this browser.
