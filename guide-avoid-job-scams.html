How to spot fake government job offers and stay safe

Last reviewed: 22 October 2026 हिंदी में पढ़ें

Government jobs are never sold. Learn the warning signs of job scams, how to check if a notice is real, and where to report fraud.

Every year, many job seekers lose money to people who promise a government job for a "fee". The most important rule is simple: a government job cannot be bought. Selection happens only through the official process written in an official notification.

Warning signs of a scam

  • Someone promises a job "without exam" or a "confirmed seat" in exchange for money.
  • You are asked to pay a "registration", "processing" or "security" fee to a person, a UPI ID or a bank account instead of on the official portal.
  • The offer comes only by WhatsApp, SMS or a social media post, with no official notification.
  • You receive an "appointment letter" without ever taking an exam or interview.
  • The website address looks almost like an official one but is slightly different.
  • You are asked for your OTP, password or full bank details.

How to check whether a job notice is real

  1. Find the notice on the recruiting body's official website — for example SSC, UPSC, the Railway Recruitment Boards or your State Public Service Commission.
  2. Official government websites usually end in .gov.in or .nic.in.
  3. Employment News, a Government of India weekly, publishes official recruitment advertisements.
  4. Pay any application fee only on the official portal named in the notification.

Protect yourself

  • Never share OTPs, passwords or bank PINs with anyone.
  • Keep copies of your certificates, but do not hand originals to agents.
  • If something feels too good to be true, talk to family or a teacher before paying anything.

If you have been cheated

Report cyber fraud quickly. Call the national cyber crime helpline 1930 or file a complaint on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. Acting fast improves the chance of stopping the money transfer. You can also go to your nearest police station.

Official sources

  • National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal
  • Staff Selection Commission (SSC)
  • Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)
  • Railway Recruitment Boards — application portal
  • Employment News
