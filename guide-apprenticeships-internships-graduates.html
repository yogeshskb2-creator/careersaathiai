Apprenticeships and internships for graduates and diploma holders: NATS and the PM Internship Scheme

Last reviewed: 22 October 2026 हिंदी में पढ़ें

Just finished your degree or diploma and need experience? NATS apprenticeships and the PM Internship Scheme offer paid, real work experience.

Many employers ask for experience, but you need a first chance to get it. Two government programmes help young people get paid work experience.

NATS 2.0 — National Apprenticeship Training Scheme

  • Run by: the Ministry of Education.
  • For: young graduates and diploma holders. The portal lists the exact conditions, including how recently you must have passed.
  • Stipend: a monthly stipend; the government's share is paid directly into your bank account through DBT.
  • Portal: the NATS 2.0 portal handles registration, openings, applications, contracts and certificates.
  • After completion: you receive an apprenticeship certificate.

NAPS — apprenticeships in companies

The National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme supports apprenticeships in companies for trade and other apprentices. It suits people who want hands-on training in a specific job role.

PM Internship Scheme

The PM Internship Scheme, from the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, offers 12-month internships in companies. The details below were published for the pilot round and may change — always check the official portal.

  • Age: 21 to 24 years.
  • Qualification: such as 10th, 12th, ITI, polytechnic diploma or graduation.
  • Other conditions: family income and family government-job conditions apply.
  • Duration: 12 months, at least half of it on the job.
  • Support (pilot): ₹5,000 per month and a one-time grant of ₹6,000.
  • Apply: register on the official portal and choose up to 5 internships.

How to make the most of it

  1. Keep your documents ready: mark sheets, degree or diploma, ID proof and bank details (only for the official portal).
  2. Prepare a one-page resume with your projects and skills.
  3. Apply only through official portals. No genuine scheme asks for a fee through an agent.
  4. During the apprenticeship or internship, learn as much as you can and ask for a reference at the end.
