Government help to start a small business: Mudra, PMEGP, Stand-Up India and PM Vishwakarma

Last reviewed: 22 October 2026 हिंदी में पढ़ें

Planning your own small business? These four government schemes can help with loans, subsidy, training and tools. Here is who each one is for.

Starting a small business — a tailoring unit, a repair shop, a food stall — usually needs some money and support. The Government of India has several schemes for this. Important: a scheme is never a guarantee. Banks and departments check every application, and no agent can "pass" your loan for a fee.

1. PM MUDRA Yojana — small loans without collateral

  • For: micro enterprises in non-farm work, including allied activities such as poultry, dairy and beekeeping.
  • Loan categories: Shishu up to ₹50,000; Kishore above ₹50,000 up to ₹5 lakh; Tarun above ₹5 lakh up to ₹10 lakh; Tarun Plus above ₹10 lakh up to ₹20 lakh for borrowers who repaid a Tarun loan well.
  • Collateral: not required.
  • Where: banks, regional rural banks, small finance banks, NBFCs and MFIs.

2. PMEGP — loan with subsidy for a new unit

  • Run by: Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC).
  • For: individuals above 18 and some groups such as SHGs, societies, co-operatives and trusts — for new micro-enterprises only.
  • Education: at least 8th pass is needed for some projects.
  • Support: a margin-money subsidy on project cost, higher for special categories and rural areas. Banks fund most of the project; your own contribution is 10% (general) or 5% (special categories).
  • Training: entrepreneurship training (EDP) is mandatory.
  • Apply: online on the KVIC PMEGP e-portal.

3. Stand-Up India — larger loans for SC/ST and women

  • For: SC/ST and/or women entrepreneurs above 18, starting a first-time (greenfield) enterprise in manufacturing, services or trading.
  • Loan: above ₹10 lakh and up to ₹1 crore, for term loan and/or working capital.
  • Condition: you must not be a defaulter to any bank.
  • Check first: the bank page we read said the scheme was extended up to FY2025. Confirm with your bank that it is still open.

4. PM Vishwakarma — for traditional artisans

  • For: artisans in 18 traditional trades, such as tailor (darzi), carpenter, mason, barber, potter, cobbler and goldsmith.
  • Benefits: PM Vishwakarma certificate and ID card; skill training with a stipend of ₹500 per day; a toolkit e-voucher of up to ₹15,000; collateral-free credit of up to ₹3 lakh at 5% interest; help with digital payments and marketing.
  • Apply: at your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC), with the documents it asks for.

Before you apply

  1. Write a one-page plan: what you will sell, to whom, your costs and your expected income.
  2. Talk to a few possible customers first.
  3. Visit your bank branch or District Industries Centre and ask which scheme fits you.
  4. Apply only on official portals or at the bank. Never pay an agent.
