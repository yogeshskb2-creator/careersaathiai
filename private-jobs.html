Tell us about your education, skills and the kind of work you want. We will show realistic private-sector roles, companies to explore, salary information with sources, and what to learn next.

How this works — please read
  • We compare your answers with what employers usually ask for common private jobs.
  • This site has no live job feed, so we never show a vacancy as "open". We give you search links to real job portals and official company careers pages.
  • Salary figures come from a named, dated source and are shown only when the data is strong enough.
  • Company facts come from public sources that we list. "Verification incomplete" means we could not check everything.
Your privacy
  • Your answers stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to us or to any company.
  • Name, PIN code and other optional fields can be left empty.
  • We never ask for Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, passwords, phone number or email.
  • You can clear your answers at any time.
